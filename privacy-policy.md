# Privacy Policy for Page Metrics

**Effective Date:** October 1, 2026  
**Application Name:** Page Metrics  
**Developer / Agency:** Social Media Hunt Agency  

---

## 1. Introduction
Page Metrics ("we", "our", or "us") is an internal analytics and agency workspace designed to help social media agencies monitor and analyze public Instagram Business and Creator page performance. This Privacy Policy explains how our application handles information when you use our web and mobile application.

## 2. Information We Collect
- **Agency Credentials:** We store secure, hashed agency administrator usernames and session tokens for authentication.
- **Instagram Page Data:** Our app uses Meta's official Instagram Graph API (Business Discovery) to retrieve publicly available metrics of authorized Instagram Business and Creator accounts (such as follower counts, reel views, like counts, and engagement ratios). We do not collect private personal data, direct messages, or private user posts.

## 3. How We Use Information
- To authenticate authorized agency staff.
- To display public analytics and performance reports for managed and tracked Instagram pages.
- To maintain session security and prevent unauthorized access.

## 4. Data Sharing & Security
- We do not sell, trade, or transfer your data to third parties.
- All data transmitted between the client application and our backend server is encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS.
- Database records are stored securely with password hashing (Argon2).

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us at support@pagemetrics.agency.
