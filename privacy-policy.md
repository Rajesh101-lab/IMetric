# IMetric Website Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** October 1, 2026  
**Website Name:** IMetric
**Developer / Agency:** Social Media Hunt Agency  

---

## 1. Introduction
IMetric ("we", "our", or "us") is a website and agency workspace designed to help social media agencies monitor public Instagram Business and Creator page performance. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the website and authenticated workspace.

## 2. Information We Collect
- **Agency Credentials:** We store secure, hashed agency administrator usernames and session tokens for authentication.
- **Instagram Page Data:** The website requests publicly available metrics, such as follower counts, reel views, and likes, through Meta's Instagram Graph API (Business Discovery). If an administrator enables the backup provider, eligible failed requests may send the requested username to SerpApi and use its response. The source is identified in the workspace. We do not request direct messages or private posts.

## 3. How We Use Information
- To authenticate authorized agency staff.
- To display analytics and performance reports for tracked Instagram pages.
- To maintain session security and prevent unauthorized access.

## 4. Data Sharing & Security
- We do not sell agency credentials or tracked-page records.
- Meta and, when enabled, SerpApi process requests needed to retrieve page metrics under their respective terms and privacy policies.
- Agency passwords are stored as Argon2 hashes. Production deployments should use HTTPS/TLS for data in transit.

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us at support@pagemetrics.agency.
